I am a postdoctoral researcher in the SAFARI Research Group at ETH Zurich. I completed my PhD in the same group, advised by Prof. Onur Mutlu, and spent one semester as a visiting researcher at the Robust Systems Group at Stanford University, working with Prof. Subhasish Mitra.

My research is at the intersection of computer architecture and computational biology. I work on (1) storage systems, large-scale bioinformatics applications, and their interactions, and (2) emerging technologies such as ultra-dense 3D integrated systems. I design high-performance, energy-efficient, and low-cost systems that make the data-intensive applications behind modern healthcare and life sciences practical at scale.

Before my PhD I obtained my master's degree from ETH Zurich, also advised by Prof. Onur Mutlu, and my bachelor's degree from the University of Tehran, advised by Prof. Ali Afzali-Kusha and Dr. Mehdi Kamal.
