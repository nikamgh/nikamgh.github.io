On the Academic Job Market

I am on the academic job market for tenure-track faculty positions. If you know of opportunities and openings, or are interested in talking about research, please reach out ({{ site.email }}). I would be happy to chat!

I am currently a senior researcher in the SAFARI Research Group at ETH Zurich, where I recently defended my PhD thesis, advised by Prof. Onur Mutlu.

My research is in computer architecture, systems, and computational biology. I am especially excited about designing high-performance, energy-efficient, low-cost, secure, and privacy-preserving computing systems for (i) widespread adoption of data-intensive applications in healthcare and life sciences, and (ii) enabling new solutions that were out of reach before, both in these domains and in other critical domains, such as AI/ML.

I also invite you to check my recent talk, “Fast, Efficient, and Low-Cost Computing Systems for Health and Life Sciences,” at the MICRO 2026 Job Candidate Showcase [Slides: pdf | ppt].

Along my research, I lead the organization of the Architecture for Health (Arch4Health) initiative with the goal of exploring the key computational challenges in health-related applications and the vital role of computer architecture in overcoming them. So far, we have organized the Arch4Health workshops at MICRO 2025, HPCA 2026, ICS 2026, and MICRO 2026, and the Sys4Health workshop at SOSP 2026.

During my PhD, I spent one semester as a visiting researcher at the Robust Systems Group at Stanford University, working with Prof. Subhasish Mitra. Before my PhD I obtained my master's degree from ETH Zurich, advised by Prof. Onur Mutlu, and my bachelor's degree from the University of Tehran, advised by Prof. Ali Afzali-Kusha and Dr. Mehdi Kamal.
